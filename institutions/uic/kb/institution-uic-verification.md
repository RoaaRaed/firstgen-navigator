# UIC Financial Aid: Verification

Roughly 25% of FAFSAs are selected for verification. At UIC the process works like this:

- Student Financial Aid and Scholarships (SFAS) emails the selected student a link to a verification worksheet. The student submits the worksheet together with supporting documents.
- SFAS emails the student when verification is complete. This generally takes 2 to 3 weeks.
- The federal processor sometimes flags a FAFSA and asks for more documents. The student may be asked for documents about citizenship, Selective Service registration, bankruptcy, loan default, loan discharge, veteran status, differences in Social Security number, name or date of birth, or federal aid overpayments. SFAS requests any of these by email.

Note: the UIC page lists which tax-year documents to send for an older aid year. Those year-specific details are left out here because they are out of date. Ask SFAS which documents are needed for the current aid year.
