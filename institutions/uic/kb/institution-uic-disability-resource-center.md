# UIC Disability Resource Center (DRC)

UIC aims to keep its campus barrier-free so that people with disabilities can use its programs, services and activities. The Disability Resource Center offers eligibility documentation, assistive technology, deaf and hard of hearing services, classroom and exam accommodations, note-taking, computing resources and transportation support.

New students start by opening the "New DRC Student" page on drc.uic.edu, which links to the registration form.
