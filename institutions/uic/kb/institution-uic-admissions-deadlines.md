# UIC Undergraduate Admissions Deadlines

## Application windows

Spring 2027 transfer applications and Fall 2027 and Spring 2027 first-year applications opened August 1, 2026. Fall 2027 transfer applications open December 15, 2026.

## Spring semester 2027

- First year: October 1, 2026.
- Transfer, on-campus programs: October 15, 2026.
- Transfer, online programs: varies. Check the program's website.
- Honors College: December 1, 2026.

## Fall semester 2027

- First year, Early Action: November 2, 2026.
- First year, Regular Decision: February 1, 2027.
- Transfer, Nursing: January 15, 2027 (priority) and April 1, 2027 (final deadline).
- Transfer, on-campus programs: April 1, 2027.
- Transfer, online programs: varies. Check the program's website.
- Business Scholars: November 2, 2026 (priority) and February 1, 2027 (final).
- GPPA: November 2, 2026.
- Honors College, first year: November 2, 2026 (priority) and February 1, 2027 (final).
- Honors College, transfer: May 3, 2027 (priority) and June 1, 2027 (final).
- Scholarship Priority, first year: November 2, 2026.

## Notes

- Applications and all supporting documents must be postmarked by the listed deadlines.
- Some transfer programs do not accept Spring applicants. Check the transfer guide for the intended major.
- Readmission, second bachelor's and nondegree applicants follow the transfer deadlines. Online program deadlines may differ.
- Honors College applicants must first apply to UIC as undergraduates.
- Early Action and GPPA materials must be electronically dated or postmarked by November 2. Checklist updates may lag because of processing.
- The Scholarship Priority deadline is a recommendation. Later applicants may still be considered.
- Chancellor's Fellows-Aim High Scholarship applications and documents must be submitted by the deadline. The FAFSA deadline for consideration is November 2, 2026.
