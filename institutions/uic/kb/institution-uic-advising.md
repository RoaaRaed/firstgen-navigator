# UIC Academic Advising (College of Liberal Arts and Sciences)

Undergraduate advising in the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences (LAS) is based in the LAS Advising Center. College advisors specialize in academic programs (majors, curriculum, minors), pre-health, pre-law and career development. Advisors also support students' emotional, cultural and developmental needs. Other UIC colleges have their own advisors.

## Services

- Assigned advisor: every undergraduate is assigned an advisor who stays with their group from orientation through graduation.
- Major and minor advising: helps continuing students understand program requirements and connects them with department opportunities.
- Pre-health advising and pre-law advising.
- Career development: self-guided assessments, webinars, exploration workshops and internship advising.

## How to schedule

- Online drop-in advising (las.uic.edu/advising/las-academic-advising-online-drop-in-availability/).
- Appointments, online and in person (go.uic.edu/homeservice).
