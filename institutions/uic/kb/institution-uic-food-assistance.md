# UIC Free Food Resources and the Flames Food Pantry

The UIC Wellness Center runs the UIC Flames Food Pantry for current UIC students. Food insecurity affects academic performance, physical health and emotional well-being, and the Wellness Center lists several free food resources.

## Free food resources

- Flames Food Pantry: donated free food for UIC students, handed out twice a week in Student Center East (wellnesscenter.uic.edu/pop-up-pantry/).
- Greater Chicago Food Depository "Find Food": helps people find food assistance and food pantries in the Chicago area.
- WIC: nutrition support for low-income pregnant people, new mothers and young children.
- SNAP: federal food benefits for low-income individuals and families (wellnesscenter.uic.edu/food-assistance/snap/).
- Too Good To Go: a mobile app that connects people with local restaurants, bakeries, cafes and grocery stores that sell surplus food at a steep discount.
- Dion's Chicago Dream Vault: gives UIC West Campus students a box of fresh produce every week through a smart locker on campus.
