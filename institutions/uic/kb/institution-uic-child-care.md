# UIC Children's Center (Campus Child Care)

The UIC Children's Center provides early childhood education and child care on campus for preschool-age children of current UIC students, faculty and staff. The center has held NAEYC accreditation since 1989.

Eligibility: preschool-age children of current UIC students, faculty and staff.
