# UIC Financial Aid: How to Apply

## FAFSA

Students complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) every year to be considered for federal aid: grants, work-study and student loans. UIC's application deadline is March 15. UIC's federal school code is 001776. Put this code on the FAFSA so UIC receives the results.

## FSA ID

A student, and a parent when one has to contribute, needs a Federal Student Aid (FSA) ID to sign the electronic FAFSA.

## Dependency status

Most undergraduates are dependent students. All graduate and professional students are independent. Dependent students report their parents' income. Living arrangements, being self-supporting, or a parent not claiming the student on taxes do not decide dependency status.

## Student Aid Report

After the FAFSA is processed, the student receives an electronic Student Aid Report (SAR) that shows the Student Aid Index (SAI). The SAI is used to decide grant eligibility. Review the SAR for mistakes and make corrections through the FAFSA website.

## Illinois RISE Act and the Alternative Application

The Retention of Illinois Students and Equity (RISE) Act was signed into law at UIC on June 21, 2019. It makes a group of students, including undocumented Illinois residents, eligible for state and institutional aid. The Alternative Application for Illinois Financial Aid is the path to the MAP Grant and UIC institutional funds for students who cannot submit the FAFSA. A student who cannot submit the application should contact the Illinois Student Assistance Commission (ISAC).
