# UIC Counseling Center

The UIC Counseling Center provides free, confidential mental health services to UIC students: clinical services, outreach and educational programs, consultation and professional training.

## Contact

- Address: 1200 West Harrison Street, 2010 SSB (MC 333), Chicago, Illinois 60607
- Phone: (312) 996-3490
- Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.

## Crisis and 24/7 support

- Free, anonymous 24/7 peer support (TogetherAll).
- 24/7 virtual mental health support (TimelyCare).

## Services

Group therapy, brief individual therapy, case management, single-session appointments, Mind-Body programs, virtual drop-in spaces and psychiatry services. Services are offered in person and virtually. To ask for a virtual appointment instead of an in-person visit, call the office or email your assigned therapist. To schedule an initial consultation, use the Counseling Center's guide on how to schedule and what to expect.
