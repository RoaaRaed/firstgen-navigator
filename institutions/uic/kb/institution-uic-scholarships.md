# UIC Scholarships

## Overview

Scholarships are gift aid that does not have to be repaid. Awards can consider academics, athletics, field of study and special talents. Students must report outside scholarships to Student Financial Aid and Scholarships (SFAS), because they can affect aid eligibility.

## Chancellor's Fellows (AIM HIGH)

These awards go to Illinois residents who are U.S. citizens or eligible non-citizens and whose family income is below a set multiple of the federal poverty guideline. Funding is limited, and meeting the criteria does not guarantee an award.

Academic criteria (the number of qualifying courses varies by year): be a graduating senior from an Illinois high school and either rank first in the class, or have an unweighted GPA of 3.70 plus qualifying honors, AP, IB (HL) or dual-credit courses, or have an unweighted GPA of 3.70 plus an ACT of 33 or an SAT of 1450.

For Fall 2027 entry: the priority deadline for the admission application is November 2, 2026, the FAFSA or RISE application deadline is November 2, 2026, and the income limit is 8 times the poverty guideline. The FAFSA or RISE application must list UIC's school code, 001776.

Renewal for continuing students: enroll in 12 or more credits in fall and spring, file the FAFSA each year, and keep a 3.0 GPA at the end of each academic year. The award renews for up to eight consecutive semesters.

## State programs (apply through ISAC)

- Golden Apple Scholars of Illinois: for high-achieving graduates who want to teach. Students can nominate themselves or be nominated by an unrelated adult. Nominations are due November 30 for the coming school year.
- Special Education Teacher Tuition Waiver (SETTW): waives tuition and most fees for Illinois residents studying for special education teaching certification. Recipients must work as special education teachers in Illinois or repay the full amount plus interest. Applications are due March 1.
- Minority Teacher of Illinois (MTI): for students who commit to teach at certified Illinois schools with at least 30% minority students, starting within one year of graduation. Applications are due March 1.
- Technology Fields Scholarship: administered by ISAC for UIC undergraduates majoring in technology fields. Awards go up to $7,500 per year for tuition, fees, and room and board.

## UIC institutional programs

Incoming undergraduates generally need only the main UIC admission application to be considered for most university scholarships. The Honors College requires a separate application.

Non-resident awards, reviewed by Admissions:

- Merit Tuition Award: for U.S. citizens or permanent residents who do not qualify for in-state tuition. GPA, test scores and major are considered.
- Tribal Nation Tuition Award: in-state tuition rates for new first-year and transfer students who are members of a tribal nation recognized by the Bureau of Indian Affairs.
- Lake County, Indiana Tuition Award: in-state tuition rates for new first-year and transfer students who live in Lake County, Indiana.

More information: UIC Scholarships (scholarships.uic.edu), UIC SnAP (snap.uic.edu), the Office of External Fellowships (oef.uic.edu) for national awards such as Goldwater, Truman and Fulbright, and the Graduate College for graduate funding.
