# UIC DACA and Undocumented Student Resources

The Rafael Cintrón Ortiz Latino Cultural Center at the University of Illinois Chicago lists resources for students who are undocumented, are DACA recipients, or come from families with mixed immigration status.

## Resources

- The Illinois RISE Act application for undocumented students (financialaid.uic.edu/aid-process/illinois-rise-act/).
- "Know Your Rights to Defend Your Rights" guides in English and in Spanish (PDF).
- Campus-wide and community resources (PDF).
- Fearless Undocumented Alliance (FUA), a student organization.
- Additional resources for undocumented students (dream.uic.edu), contact Tanya Cabrera, Office of Diversity, tcabrera@uic.edu.
- Undocumented Student Resources (PDF).
