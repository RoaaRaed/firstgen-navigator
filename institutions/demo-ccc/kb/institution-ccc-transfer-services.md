## City Colleges Transfer Centers are here to help you take the next step in your education.

### Find your future with CCC:

- Save thousands of dollars on your education by taking transferrable classes here, then finishing your bachelor’s degree at a four-year institution.
- Take advantage of **guaranteed admissions** from City Colleges to an array of colleges and universities across the country and scholarships designed specifically for transfer students.
- City Colleges is part of the **[Illinois Articulation Initiative (IAI)](https://www.ccc.edu/departments/Pages/Illinois-Articulation-Initiative-(IAI).aspx)**, which means when you take IAI classes here, they are guaranteed to transfer to any IAI college or university across the state. You can find a list of our IAI approved courses in our [Academic Catalog](https://catalog.ccc.edu/iai-gecc/).

#### TABLE KEY

**Partnership Type:**

Admissions Partnerships

- Agreements that allow City Colleges of Chicago students to receive guaranteed admission to a partner university, provided all eligibility requirements are met by the time they complete their associate degree. These partnerships are designed to support a seamless transfer process.

Open Access Transfer

- Universities offer guaranteed admissions to transfer students, as long as they meet all the qualifications listed. This partnership is not tied to CCC students, transfer students from any college can apply as long as they meet the criteria.

Star Scholarship

- Star Scholars have access to scholarships and other benefits when transferring to partner four-year institutions. It is important that students follow the criteria set by the university, to be guaranteed the funding.

Program Articulation

- Structured academic pathways that align specific CCC programs with bachelor’s degree programs at partner universities, helping ensure efficient credit transfer and timely degree completion.

**Affiliation:**

HBCU

- Historically Black Colleges and University. Known for strong support systems, cultural significance, and producing a large share of Black professionals.

HSI

- Institution that enroll at least 25% Latine students.

PBI

- Institution that enroll at least 40% Black students.

Private

- Private colleges/universities are higher education institutions that are primarily funded through tuition fees, donations, and endowments from private sources, such as individuals, foundations, and corporations.

Public

- Public colleges/universities are higher education institutions that are primarily funded by state governments.

**Modality:**

Modality: In-Person

- Institution offers the majority of courses face-to-face on a physical campus.

Modality: Both in-person & Online

- Institution offers courses face-to-face on a physical campus and virtually.

Modality: Online

- Institution offers majority of courses virtually.

**Scholarship Type:**

University

- Scholarships offered and funded directly by a college or university. These awards are typically available to students who apply and are admitted to that institution and may be based on academic achievement, financial need, or participation in specific programs.

External

- Scholarships provided by organizations outside of colleges and universities, such as foundations, nonprofits, or corporations. These opportunities require a separate application and may be used at a variety of institutions, depending on the scholarship guidelines.

Star Scholarship

- Star Scholars have access to scholarships and other benefits when transferring to partner four-year institutions. It is important that students follow the criteria set by the university, to be guaranteed the funding.

Important Tips

- Students are encouraged to apply to both university and external scholarships to maximize funding opportunities.

## Credit for Prior Learning (CPL)

**[Credit for Prior Learning (CPL)](https://colleges.ccc.edu/cpl/)** also known as Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) is learning you’ve done outside the classroom, including work experience, employer training, military experience, independent study or research, non-collegiate learning, professional certifications, civic activities, volunteer service, and more.
