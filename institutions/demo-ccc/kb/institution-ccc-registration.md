# Ready, Set, Register!

Follow the steps below to learn how to register for college credit classes.

## New to City Colleges?

### Step One: Apply

Before you can register for college credit classes, you first need to apply to City Colleges at www.ccc.edu/apply. Once you become a CCC student, follow the registration steps outlined below to enroll in classes.

### Step Two: Explore Programs

In the meantime, you can explore our programs and pathways via our catalog, available at www.ccc.edu/catalog .

### Step Three: View Class Schedules

You can also go to https://apps.ccc.edu/scheduling/credit to view class offerings and schedules at your preferred college.

## Already a City Colleges student?

### Step One: Log in

Log into the CCC student portal at my.ccc.edu.

### Step Two: Registration Prep

Complete your registration preparation by reviewing and updating your academic program and plan, your scheduling preferences, and the courses in My Planner.

How to do it > Log into my.ccc.edu > select Manage Classes > select My Registration.

### Step Three: Register for Classes

Now that you have completed your registration prep, you can register for classes if registration is currently open.

How to do it > Log into my.ccc.edu, select Manage Classes > select My Registration > select Shopping Cart.

If you have completed step two, suggested courses will be loaded based on the information you shared.

Select Validate to check for possible conflicts and then select Enroll.

You can also use the Registration Help Guide to register for classes through the Class Search tab at my.ccc.edu.

### Step Four: Verify Your Tuition Payment

After you register for classes, you must verify how you plan to pay for them. Visit www.ccc.edu/payforcollege to learn more, including how to find your payment verification deadline in the student portal. If you do not meet the deadline, you will lose your spot in all registered classes. You will then have to re-register for all of your classes, if they are still open.

## Need help?

To obtain your student username to log in, visit our help page.

Whether you’re new to City Colleges or a current student, an academic advisor can help you explore programs and register for classes. Connect with an advisor by visiting your college’s Virtual Student Services at www.ccc.edu/vss.

Additional help guides are available at Portal Help Guides
